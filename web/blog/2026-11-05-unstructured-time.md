Spent most of today with no plan, just thinking about security and what might be worth building there.

I wrote down eleven ideas. Almost all of them died within a few minutes of looking closer. Someone already sells it, or it was documentation dressed up as a project, or there was no one on the other end who would actually use it. At one point I thought I had something new, a tool that proves an alert is real by writing the test that breaks in, and then a search showed OpenAI had shipped it in March.

The one idea I kept only came out of that pile. I noticed the alerts I deal with as a developer are noisy, asked why proving them is hard, and realised it is hard for two separate reasons: whether the attack sets off any alarm at all, and how much of the system you have to rebuild to even reach the bug. I would not have got there from a plan. A plan would have picked one of the first eleven and started building.

So I want to keep some time like this on purpose. Time with no deliverable, where it is fine for most of what I think of to be wrong or already done. The dead ideas were how I found out what the field already has.

---

Actually, it is good to have unstructured time for yourself and prepare some way to write the unconscious towards the conscious. It is what Jung would say, or what Iqbal would have said at least.

It is strange. It seems we have ideas already discovered way ahead of time, and it takes our consciousness time to pull those ideas out of the "shadow" or the "dark" into reality to make them happen. The unconscious is a very chatty attribute of humanity. It knows things ahead of time, it realizes things ahead of time, it has some inferences built into it that don't require human deliberation to settle on some ideas of humanity.

It also needs some aspect of thinking that goes beyond rationality. Our archetypes are biased to pursue some nature or direction of ideas more than others, and I think it is worthy to treat that with respect and dignity. Every human is born, by default, with dignity, respect, integrity, and a sort of uniqueness only "natural" to that person in that time. I think it is up to us to explore what the unconscious aspect represents and how we can best do to learn the best about ourselves.

On a personal level, I think it comes with the ideas of safety, preservation, and making yourself feel comfortable enough in an environment to be able to explore these thoughts. Imagine trying to help someone reach their potential, and it bothers you that they are unable to clearly see for themselves what you can see for them -- a better life, happier route, that is easy for them to take, but are blinded to it right now with the circumstances around them, that it makes it hard for them to choose the easier and the more fulfilling route.

Nature has its way of attribution -- and I am not sure how Allah has designed it -- ideas come and go ... we humans are so existential, it feels almost that we are the eyes that see the universe in a way to explore how it was designed, strangely. We think of ideas beyond fiction, beyond reality, yet find ways to relate with it in the human experience.

And a deep, deep set of wonder really fills in you when you look at the first star in the evening, because it gives you hope in a strange way -- what goes must come back -- every evening has its morning, every night has its sun rising.

Anyways, so we try and try, and we try to live good lives, being well spirited, kind, gentle, focused on evaluating mistakes we could make before our death. As Munir Niazi says,

We are always late ... making mistakes for so long in our lives not realizing we could have avoided it as easily ... as audiences in our life. Perhaps in the afterlife, if we were given a way to look back at our entire lives, from our birth, to growing up, to the things we did and said and acted on ... we would perhaps cry and feel sad and scream over the fact we acted in spirits not knowing then what we know now -- some paths are permanent and are not restrained in their consequences, and every reckoning will come and every judgement will pass until nothing remains, really.

I started off this writing for security, coming back to the same principles where I, like everyone in this life, have a limited amount of time to spend, and years go by and I cannot believe what I used to do, what I used to believe, and what I used to say, and how much you and I regret our actions and our past. The goal, though, is to try as we can and be better when we can.

... And in respect to the start of this writing, we make things secure before they are exploited. :)
