Advisor Atlas

A map of faculty and researchers around the world, for students looking for a PhD advisor and a way to pay for the degree.

v2, October 2026
Open the map Source code on GitHub

How a student uses it

Built first for students in Pakistan applying abroad, who usually do this with spreadsheets and guesswork. A student describes what they want to research and follows four stops.

  1. 1

    Say what you want to research

    Type a goal such as "mechanistic interpretability", or narrow by research area: 27 in computer science, plus 14 in the sciences, engineering and medicine. Search reads meaning, not just keywords.

  2. 2

    See where the faculty are

    Universities on a map, sized by how many faculty work in your areas, with the professors whose recent work is closest to your goal.

  3. 3

    Read their recent work

    Each professor's recent papers, closest to your search first, and their research grants, so you can write to them about something they're working on now.

  4. 4

    Find the money

    Which faculty hold active grants (they hire research assistants), plus scholarships for studying in each university's country, including the DAAD database for Germany.

Limits and what's next

Known limits

  • Outside computer science, researchers are a first cut. They come from OpenAlex for US R1 universities only, chosen by citations; they are not a verified faculty list.
  • Germany, Canada, China, Korea and India lack national grant data. Germany's DFG does not allow collection;
  • France is thin in CSRankings itself, so few French faculty can be linked.
  • Grants miss industry and university funding, so "no active grant" never means "no funding".

Next

  • Put it in front of 10 students and change it based on what they say.
  • Publish it at a public address.
  • More fields and countries for researchers outside computer science.
