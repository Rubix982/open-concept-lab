Advisor Atlas

A map of professors and researchers around the world, for students looking for a PhD advisor and a way to pay for the degree.

Open the map · Take the tour · Source code

Who it is for

Students applying for a PhD, first of all students in Pakistan applying abroad, who usually do this with spreadsheets and guesswork.

How to use it

  1. Say what you want to research, in your own words. The search reads meaning, not only keywords. You can also pick research areas.
  2. See who works on it and where. Universities on a map, and the people whose recent work is closest to your search.
  3. Read a profile. Their recent papers, their grants, who they work with, and a short "before you write" checklist.
  4. Find the money. Who holds grants (they hire research assistants), where the money for your topic goes, funded PhD programmes, and scholarships for each country.

What is in it

  • 198,570 professors and researchers at 700 universities in 61 countries; 4,708 of them at 25 universities in Pakistan
  • Computer science faculty from CSRankings (18,015); researchers in 25 other fields (about 300 research areas, from cardiology to economics) from OpenAlex (180,555)
  • 1.26 million recent papers, 1.1 million research grants from 42 funders (183,002 running now), all searchable by meaning
  • 95 scholarship programmes

Limits

  • Researchers outside computer science come from OpenAlex and are chosen by citations. They are not a checked list of faculty.
  • Germany has no national grant data here yet; India's covers medical research only (ICMR), and Korea's mostly the humanities and social sciences.
  • A grant is linked to a person only when the name and the university both match, so some grants show no one even when the researcher is listed.
  • Industry and university funding is not included, so "no grant" does not mean "no funding".
  • Always check a professor's own page before writing to them.
