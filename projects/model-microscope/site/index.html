A language model, opened up three ways on the same prompt: what it would say if it stopped at each layer, which words it looks back at, and which parts actually hold the fact. Pick a case on the left.

GPT-2 medium (355M parameters, 24 layers). Everything was computed ahead of time by trace.py; nothing runs in your browser.

Methods: the logit lens (nostalgebraist, 2020) reads each layer's state through the model's own final layer norm and output matrix. Causal tracing follows Meng et al., Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT (ROME, 2022): the noised words get Gaussian noise at three times the embedding spread, ten noise draws per cell, and an MLP or attention restoration covers a window of ten layers around the one shown. Attention is shown as recorded; the first word soaking up attention is a known habit of these models (an "attention sink"), not a finding about the prompt.
