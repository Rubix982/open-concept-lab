## Defect 1 is two defects wearing one label

E-003 found Pass 1 assumed a draft existed, and proposed a drafting mode: audit the
claims before writing prose. On a design that patch does not help, **because a
design has no claims.** Its assertions are about what will be measured, and "we
will sweep eight layers" cannot be false.

The substitution that worked:

> *If this assumption is wrong, does the experiment still measure what it claims?*

That does Pass 1's job — separating load-bearing from decorative — but it is a
different question, not a re-ordering of the same one. An assumption that survives
being wrong is fine; one that does not is a confound that must be controlled before
the ticket opens.

So the fix is **three Pass 1 modes, not two**: revise (audit paragraphs), draft
(audit claims), design (audit assumptions).

## What the pass actually found — and it is not a writing improvement

Filling the assumption table produced a **gap in a claim published four hours ago**.

Every control behind [E-017]'s "any prompt containing the subject receives 93–100%"
is a same-subject control: different relation, late clause, possessive, long
preamble. A matched different-subject probe — different person, same relation, same
form — has never been measured. The project's only non-subject datum, `inner_2`, is
a different entity type answering a different question.

Without it, *"any prompt containing the subject receives 93–100%"* is not separated
from *"any prompt receives 93–100%"*.

And [E-016]'s own anisotropy number is the reason to worry rather than shrug:
participation ratio 26.5 of 2048 means layer-5 keys occupy a ~26-dimensional
manifold, and vectors confined to a narrow subspace have substantial cosine **by
construction**. The floor may be high.

**That is a research finding produced by a writing pass.** I did not expect the
skill to do this and the skill does not claim it. It fell out of asking "if this
assumption is wrong, does the experiment survive?" of the assumption that a
coefficient near 1 indicates *subject-specific* pinning.

It also spawned T-076 and reordered the work: the one-measurement control should
run **before** the eight-layer sweep, because it could retract the claim the sweep
is built to extend.

## The ranking effect, confirmed

Same as E-003. The audit put the control assumption (A3) on top, which made the
different-subject control the sweep's **primary purpose** rather than a control
attached to it. The layer question — the one the thread asked — is now second.

Twice now, on two document types, the audit's effect has been to reorder rather
than to filter. `SKILL.md` describes it only as a filter — and on both uses the
filtering caught zero unfalsifiable claims while the reordering changed the
section. **The skill documents the half that did nothing.**

## What went unused, and one thing that was missing

`voice.md`: not opened, second time. The register here came from the surrounding
document, as in E-003. It is now hard to argue it earns its slot for anything but
drafting from nothing.

`mathematics.md`: used, and it paid. The `(k·u)/(u·k*)` reduction to
`(k·k*)/(k*·k*)` is stated in words first — "a function of two keys and nothing
else" — before the algebra, and the ladder is why the section can claim the sweep

**Missing: nothing in the skill addresses feasibility.** The design has to price an
NDIF dependency that fails ~50% of calls, and that section is load-bearing — it
changes the artifact design (resumable, checkpointed per layer). No pass asks
"can this actually be run?" For research writing that is a real hole, and it is
lens 8 of the design protocol that already exists in `CLAUDE.md`. The skill and
the design protocol overlap and neither references the other.

## Postscript — what the lint caught, both times

Two live uses, and on each the lint's only hit was **an unquantified superlative in
my own prose**: "the most promising place left to look" in E-003, "plausibly the
main benefit" here. Both times the fix was to supply the criterion, and both times
the replacement was more specific than what it replaced.

At corpus scale that rule gives only a 2x separation (0.0 / 1.6 / 3.1 per 10k) and
is not a quality gate. As a **drafting** check on my own output it has a 2-for-2
hit rate with no false positives across 2,700 words. Those are different jobs and
the second one is going better than the measurement predicted.
