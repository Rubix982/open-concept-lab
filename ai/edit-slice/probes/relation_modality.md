**These labels are a judgement call and are published to be contested.** The
criterion is *time-rigidity*: can a later event reconcile the old and new value?
If yes the relation is **mutable** — the tower was moved, the job changed — and
no contradiction arises, so no orphan is possible in principle. If no, the
relation is **rigid** and an orphan is forced. Labels are applied per **relation
type**, never per fact; that is what keeps this out of the circularity in
session-2026-09-08 §5.

Disagree with a row? Change `MODALITY` in `src/relation_inventory.py` and re-run.
The headline number moves with it, which is the point.

## Reading

**7,770 rigid edits — 35.4% of CounterFact.** The T-023 fear was that CounterFact
is so dominated by mutable relations that a naive 50-edit sample would draw almost
entirely from the row where orphaning cannot occur, measure a null, and be
misread as "the asymmetry is not there." That fear is **unfounded**: rigid edits
outnumber the pilot's needs by two orders of magnitude, and 12,073 mutable edits
are available for the matched control group.

**The gate is passed. The pilot's edit selection must still be stratified** — by
modality first, and reported per relation, since P30 alone contributes 12% of the
rigid pool and could otherwise dominate.

**9.5% ambiguous is reported, not cleaned.** Three relations resist the binary:
`P176` manufacturer (fixed for an artifact, mutable for a product line), `P136`
genre (fixed for a work, changeable for a person, and CounterFact's prompt is
person-oriented), `P641` sport. Per T-027 the ambiguous fraction is itself a
result — it measures how often the binary we imposed does not fit.

**An observation worth keeping.** CounterFact's own prompt wording sometimes
carries the modality: `P449` "was released on", `P364` "The original language of
{} was", `P495` "created in" all encode a creation event in the surface form. So
rigidity is partly recoverable from the template, not only from the property.
That is a cheap cross-check on the labels and possibly a way to extend them to
vocabularies we have not hand-labelled.

**Threat left open.** Rigidity is necessary for an orphan, not sufficient. A
rigid relation with no mined grounds in the KG yields no kernel and so no probe.
Rigid-relation coverage in the DBpedia rule set is unmeasured and is E-001's job.
